# Privacy Policy — Playlist & Subs Cleaner for YouTube

_Last updated: October 2026_

**Playlist & Subs Cleaner for YouTube does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.**

## What the extension accesses
To do its job, the extension reads, on youtube.com pages you open, the lists you ask it to clean: your Liked videos, your Watch later playlist and your subscriptions (video IDs, titles, channel names, thumbnails). It uses your existing, already logged-in YouTube session to remove or restore items, exactly as if you clicked the buttons yourself.

## What happens to that data
- It is processed **only inside your browser**, on your computer.
- It is **never sent to the developer or to any third party**. The only network requests the extension makes are to `youtube.com`, the same site you are already using.
- The backup file is created by your browser and saved to your own Downloads folder. The extension does not upload it anywhere and keeps no copy.
- The extension stores nothing in the browser (no cookies, no local storage, no sync storage) and uses no analytics, telemetry, advertising or remote code.

## Permissions
- `scripting` and access to `https://www.youtube.com/*`: needed to show the cleanup window on YouTube pages and to run the actions you request there. Nothing else is accessed.
